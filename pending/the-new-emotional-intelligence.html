The book

The New Emotional Intelligence

By Dr. Travis Bradberry, coauthor of Emotional Intelligence 2.0

The New Emotional Intelligence is the new book by Dr. Travis Bradberry, the world's bestselling emotional intelligence author. It gives you a proven, step-by-step program, built on the latest breakthroughs in psychology and neuroscience, to raise your EQ.

What is The New Emotional Intelligence about?

The world is changing rapidly, and emotional intelligence (EQ) is more important now than ever. As times change, so should your approach to increasing your EQ.

The book answers that call with a powerful way to increase your EQ. It applies the latest breakthroughs in psychology and neuroscience to a proven, step-by-step program you can start using today.

What's inside the book?

  • 60 new super strategies: practical, science-backed techniques you can put to work today to raise each of your EQ skills.
  • The Emotional Intelligence Test™: a unique passcode to measure your EQ and pinpoint exactly where to focus first.
  • Video e-learning and coaching: a video-based e-learning and online coaching plan to guide your growth, step by step.

Which emotional intelligence skills does it build?

The program is built around the four core skills of emotional intelligence:

  • Self-awareness: accurately perceive your emotions in the moment and understand what drives them.
  • Self-management: use that awareness to stay flexible and direct your behavior positively.
  • Social awareness: read the emotions of others and grasp what is really going on around you.
  • Relationship management: use awareness of emotions to manage interactions and build stronger bonds.

Who is the book for?

It is for anyone who wants to raise their EQ. Emotional intelligence is not fixed: it is a skill anyone can build at any age. It shapes how you handle stress, make decisions, lead, and connect with others, so the book is useful at work and at home.

How is it different from Emotional Intelligence 2.0?

Emotional Intelligence 2.0 is the massive bestseller Dr. Bradberry coauthored. The New Emotional Intelligence is his new book. It brings 60 new strategies, the Emotional Intelligence Test, and a video-based e-learning and online coaching plan, and it applies the latest breakthroughs in psychology and neuroscience for a changing world.

Who wrote it?

Dr. Travis Bradberry is the world's bestselling emotional intelligence author. His work has sold over 5 million books and is available in more than 150 countries. He holds a dual Ph.D. in Clinical and Industrial-Organizational psychology and a Bachelor of Science in Clinical Psychology from UC San Diego, and he is a LinkedIn Top Voice with 2.6 million followers.
